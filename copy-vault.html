WHY THIS ONE, NOT A FREE GENERATOR

• free sites give you one outline with no colour names at all. you get eighteen outlines including two gridded, a measured swatch card, and two more pages of step-by-step guidance for every single colour in your painting.

• free sites give you no hex values, so you cannot match a single colour to a single marker without eyeballing it off a screen. every colour here comes with its hex and RGB.

• free sites drop the numbers on regions too small to hold them. this one draws a pointer line to every one.

• the colours run lightest to darkest, which is the order markers and pencils actually want. you can always go darker over a light colour, you can never go lighter. the numbering does that thinking for you.

GOOD TO KNOW BEFORE YOU PRINT (markers)

• print the swatch card first, on the exact paper you plan to use, and scribble a marker on it. that one test tells you two things at once: which of your markers match, and whether your printer's ink smears. some inkjet inks lift when alcohol hits them; laser and toner prints don't move. if yours smears, a print shop or a laser printer solves it for a dollar or two.

• marker paper or smooth cardstock beats copy paper. alcohol goes straight through thin sheets, so put a scrap behind it either way.

• coloured pencils are happiest on toothier cardstock, and they want the medium outline, not the faint one.
